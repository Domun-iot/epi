## Body composition

The community Mi formulae and parameters are adapted from [lolouk44/xiaomi_mi_scale](https://github.com/lolouk44/xiaomi_mi_scale/tree/e9db989d21b23602a462338a285478658cbaa137), © 2020 lolouk44, under [MIT](licenses/xiaomi_mi_scale-MIT.txt). Upstream work also credits syssi and prototux/wiecosystem. The numerical solver in `shared/estimated-impedance.cjs` is an éPI contribution.

Sun, Janssen, Deurenberg, CUN-BAE, Watson, Hume–Weyers, Mifflin–St Jeor, Harris–Benedict, Owen and Cunningham methods implement equations published by their authors. Bibliographic references and links per method are in the [CLI guide](cli/README.md#comparing-body-composition-estimates) and interface. BMI is the weight/height² relationship.
